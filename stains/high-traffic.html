Carpet

High Traffic Areas

Traffic lanes usually look dirty because soil gets ground deep into the fibers. The key is to vacuum well, pre-treat, then rinse/extract so residue doesn’t re-soil.

What you’ll need

  • Vacuum (with beater brush if possible)
  • Carpet pre-spray or a mild carpet-safe cleaner
  • Soft carpet brush
  • Clean white towels
  • Warm water in a spray bottle

Optional: a wet/dry shop vac or carpet extractor for best results.

Step-by-step

  1. Vacuum slowly, twice. Most “stains” in traffic lanes are dry soil.
  2. Pre-spray the lane lightly (don’t soak). Let it dwell 5–10 minutes.
  3. Agitate gently with a soft brush in multiple directions.
  4. Rinse with a light mist of warm water.
  5. Blot / extract moisture using towels or an extractor.
  6. Dry fast. Use a fan if you have one.

Avoid this

  • Over-wetting (can cause wicking or backing issues)
  • Leaving cleaner residue (it attracts new dirt)
  • Scrubbing aggressively (can fuzz fibers)
