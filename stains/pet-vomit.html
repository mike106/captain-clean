Carpet

Pet Vomit

Vomit can be acidic and can discolor carpet if left. Remove solids first, then clean and rinse thoroughly.

What you’ll need

  • Gloves + paper towels
  • White towels
  • Mild soap solution or carpet spot cleaner
  • Warm water (spray bottle)

Step-by-step

  1. Remove solids carefully with paper towels.
  2. Blot the remaining moisture (don’t smear).
  3. Apply a small amount of spot cleaner, let dwell 3–5 minutes.
  4. Blot with a clean towel; repeat as needed.
  5. Rinse lightly with warm water and blot again.
  6. Dry with a fan to prevent odor.

Avoid this

  • Over-wetting (can wick into padding)
  • Scrubbing hard (spreads it and damages fibers)
  • Leaving cleaner behind (sticky residue)
