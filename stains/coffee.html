Coffee Stain

Coffee is a tannin stain. The winning move is to blot, then flush + blot repeatedly—don’t soak.

What you’ll need

  • White towels
  • Warm water (spray bottle)
  • Mild dish soap or carpet-safe spot cleaner

Step-by-step

  1. Blot to remove liquid.
  2. Mist warm water to dilute, then blot again.
  3. Apply a small amount of soap solution.
  4. Blot (don’t scrub). Repeat light mist + blot cycles.
  5. Final rinse (water only) and blot dry.

Avoid this

  • Over-wetting (can cause wicking rings)
  • Scrubbing aggressively
  • Leaving cleaner residue
