Carpet & Fabric

Magic Marker

Marker ink can spread fast. Use small amounts, blot repeatedly, and work from the outside edge inward.

What you’ll need

  • White towels / cotton pads
  • Rubbing alcohol (70%+), used sparingly
  • Warm water for rinsing
  • Mild soap solution

Test first in a hidden spot. Solvents can affect some dyes.

Step-by-step

  1. Blot dry ink (don’t rub).
  2. Dab a small amount of rubbing alcohol onto a towel (not directly onto carpet).
  3. Blot the stain gently. Move to clean towel sections often.
  4. Repeat in short cycles until transfer slows.
  5. Rinse lightly with warm water and blot dry.
  6. If needed, follow with a tiny amount of soap solution, then rinse again.

Avoid this

  • Pouring solvent directly on the carpet (spreads and can damage backing)
  • Scrubbing (drives ink deeper)
  • Mixing harsh chemicals
