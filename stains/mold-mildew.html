Carpet & Surfaces

Mold / Mildew

Here’s the truth: if moisture isn’t fixed, mold returns. Clean only after you stop the source and dry fast.

What you’ll need

  • Gloves + mask, good ventilation
  • Fans / dehumidifier (drying is the priority)
  • Mild cleaner appropriate to the surface
  • White towels

If carpet padding is wet or moldy, DIY cleaning often isn’t enough.

Step-by-step

  1. Stop the moisture. Leak, humidity, wet slab—fix it first.
  2. Dry aggressively. Fans + dehumidifier for 24–48 hours if needed.
  3. Surface clean visible mildew with a mild cleaner (follow label).
  4. Blot/rinse as appropriate for the surface and dry again.
  5. Monitor. If odor returns, moisture remains or contamination is deeper.

Avoid this

  • “Cover-up” sprays without drying the area
  • Soaking carpet to “wash it out” (makes it worse)
  • Ignoring musty odor (it’s a warning sign)

If there’s a large area, heavy odor, or anyone is sensitive to mold, call a professional.
