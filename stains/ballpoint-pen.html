Carpet & Fabric

Ballpoint Pen

Ballpoint ink is often oil-based. Small, repeated blotting beats aggressive scrubbing every time.

What you’ll need

  • White towels / cotton pads
  • Rubbing alcohol (use sparingly)
  • Warm water for rinsing
  • Mild soap solution

Test first on an unseen area.

Step-by-step

  1. Blot any excess ink immediately.
  2. Apply alcohol to a towel (not directly to carpet).
  3. Blot gently and rotate to a clean section often.
  4. When ink transfer slows, rinse lightly with warm water and blot.
  5. Finish with a tiny amount of soap solution, then rinse again.
  6. Dry with a fan.

Avoid this

  • Soaking the area (spreads ink into padding)
  • Scrubbing hard
  • Using multiple strong chemicals together
