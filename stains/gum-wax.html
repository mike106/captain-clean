Chewing Gum / Wax Removal

For gum and wax, the best method is usually: harden → chip → clean residue. Heat is a last resort and must be controlled.

What you’ll need

  • Ice cubes in a bag (or a cold pack)
  • Dull butter knife / spoon edge
  • White towels
  • Carpet spot cleaner (for residue)

Step-by-step

  1. Freeze it. Press ice bag on gum/wax for 2–5 minutes until hard.
  2. Chip carefully with a dull edge. Don’t yank fibers.
  3. Blot residue with a small amount of spot cleaner.
  4. Rinse lightly with water and blot dry.
  5. Dry with a fan.

Avoid this

  • Over-heating (can melt wax into backing)
  • Pulling or tearing fibers
  • Using sharp blades (risk of carpet damage)
