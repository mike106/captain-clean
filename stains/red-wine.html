Red Wine Stain

Wine spreads fast. Your job is to blot, then flush and lift. The worst mistake is scrubbing it deeper.

What you’ll need

  • White towels / paper towels
  • Cold water (spray bottle)
  • Mild dish soap (clear) or carpet-safe spot cleaner

Step-by-step

  1. Blot immediately to remove as much liquid as possible.
  2. Mist with cold water to dilute, then blot again.
  3. Mix a small amount of dish soap + water. Apply lightly.
  4. Blot from outside-in to avoid spreading.
  5. Rinse with a light mist of water, then blot until towels come up clean.
  6. Dry with a fan.

Avoid this

  • Hot water (can set tannins)
  • Scrubbing hard
  • Leaving soap residue (it re-attracts soil)
